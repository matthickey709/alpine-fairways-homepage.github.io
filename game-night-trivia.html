Game Night Trivia

Pick a category. Lock in your answer. Prove it.

The idea

The best part of any game night is the moment two people are absolutely certain they're right. Game Night Trivia is a Jeopardy-style board built for exactly that: pick a category, choose your value, everyone answers, and find out who actually knew it.

How it plays

A grid of categories and escalating point values goes up on the TV. The host reads each clue, everyone types their answer on their phone, and the host judges: full points, a deduction, or partial credit for the answer that was nearly right. Double Jeopardy, Final Jeopardy with secret wagers, and team play are there when you want them. Play on the couch, across the table, or over a video call. It works wherever the argument is happening.

Why we built it

We wanted a trivia game that felt like the show without the clutter: fast to start, easy to run, and satisfying to win.
