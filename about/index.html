About

Alpine Fairways Holdings Inc.

An independent holding company in St. John's, Newfoundland & Labrador.

Alpine Fairways Holdings builds and runs small brands and software applications. The company is run by Matt Hickey, a computer engineer by training. Most of what we make started as something we wanted for our own nights out, golf rounds, and ski days.

What we make

  • Game Night Trivia: a Jeopardy-style trivia game. The host runs the board and everyone answers from their phone.
  • Proper Drop: an app for rating pints of Guinness and finding the best pour in town.

We also ran Alpine Fairways Golf & Ski Club, an apparel line for people who play both seasons. It has since wound down.

How we build

Small and quick. Proper Drop went from idea to working app in time for a pub crawl, built on Vite and Firebase with a lot of help from AI coding tools. Game Night Trivia runs entirely on Cloudflare's free tier. We'd rather ship something useful, see how people actually use it, and improve it than plan forever. The notes cover how some of it works.

Where we are

St. John's, Newfoundland & Labrador, Canada. Everything we make is designed here.
