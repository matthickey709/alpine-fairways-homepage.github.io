Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit alpinefairways.ca, who collects it, and the choices you have. The site is run by Alpine Fairways Holdings Inc., St. John's, Newfoundland & Labrador, Canada ("we", "us").

The short version

  • This site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no comments. We don't ask you for personal information here.
  • Our web host and font provider receive your IP address when your browser loads a page, as with any website.
  • We are applying to show ads from Google AdSense. Third parties, including Google, use cookies to serve ads, and you can opt out of personalized advertising (see Google AdSense and advertising cookies).
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Scope: our apps have their own policies

This policy covers alpinefairways.ca only. Our apps run on their own domains and handle sign-in and saved data, so they have their own privacy terms:

  • Proper Drop (properdrop.ca): see the Proper Drop privacy policy.
  • Game Night Trivia (trivia.alpinefairways.ca): uses Google Sign-In so your trivia boards are saved to your account and private to you. Questions about that data can be sent to the address below.

Information collected when you visit

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When your browser requests a page, GitHub receives standard request information, including your IP address, browser type, and the page requested, and may keep it in logs for security and operating the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers to download them, which shares your IP address and browser details with Google. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed for that conversation and our records.

Analytics

We do not currently run an analytics service on alpinefairways.ca, and this site sets no analytics cookies. We don't track which pages you read or build a profile of you.

If we add an analytics service in the future (for example, Google Analytics), we will update this section before it goes live to say which service we use, what it collects (typically pages viewed, referring site, approximate location derived from IP address, and device and browser type), what cookies it sets, and how to opt out.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

This site is applying to use Google AdSense to show ads. Ads help pay for the free games and apps we build. No ads or ad code are on the site yet; once ads are running:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Opting out. You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google in Google's ad settings. You can opt out of other third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. If you opt out you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your browsing.

Visitors in the EU, UK, and Switzerland. Before ads go live, we will add a consent banner so that visitors in these regions can choose whether to allow advertising cookies.

To learn more, read how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Managing cookies. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking cookies won't stop this site from working: every page is plain HTML and reads fine without cookies or JavaScript.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw consent. Depending on where you live, you may have similar or additional rights. Email us using the address below and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change, for example before turning on analytics, and change the "Last updated" date above.
