The Inaugural St. John's Guinness Tour

November 29, 2025 · Matthew Hickey

The inspiration for a Guinness rating app came from a plan to drive around St. John's in search of the best pint of Guinness. For the first iteration, the tour visited 8 bars, rating pints along the way with Proper Drop.

The podium finishers

1st · Peter Easton Pub

The clear winner of the evening. The Peter Easton Pub delivered fantastic Guinness. The temperature was perfect, cold enough to be refreshing, and the glasses were served chilled. It was the first visit for many folks on the tour, and it left a fantastic first impression. The live music session was the cherry on top.

2nd · Big Ben's

A 50-plus-year-old suburban pub that lived up to its reputation. Big Ben's was cozy and already decorated for Christmas. The pints were cold, smooth, and tasty, and much of the group was able to sit together at a larger table in the back. This was an early stop on the tour, and it was immediately a contender.

3rd · Bally Haly Country Club

Third place goes to Bally Haly Country Club, where the night began with a meal. It was on the tour because many of the people on it are members at the club and knew its reputation for pouring a good pint. It was a great start to the night, and many had two pints there.

What's next

The group looks forward to running the tour again and trying more pubs. Proper Drop will keep being used throughout the year, since anyone can rate a pint at any time, but this was the tour that started the idea.
