Proper Drop

Rate every pint of Guinness. Find the best pour in town.

The idea

Not all Guinness is created equal. The same beer poured two blocks apart can be a religious experience or a genuine disappointment, and until now that knowledge lived only in your memory and a few strong opinions. Proper Drop turns it into a number. Rate the pint in front of you, see how the bar stacks up, and never waste a pour again.

How it works

Sit down, order a pint, and give it a score out of 10. One number, honestly earned. If you want to be thorough, switch to an advanced rating and score the head, temperature, taste, and texture separately, and add photos of the pour. Your rating posts to a live feed, so when someone lands a perfect 10 downtown, everyone sees it instantly. Over time the scores add up into a bar leaderboard you can filter by country, region, or city: a map of where to drink and where not to.

Built for the crawl

Proper Drop started before a pub crawl and shipped fast because of it. It's lightweight, it's real-time, it installs on your phone's home screen, and it settles arguments.
