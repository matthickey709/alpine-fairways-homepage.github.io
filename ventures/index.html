Current portfolio

Two consumer web applications, designed, built, and operated by Alpine Fairways Holdings.

Game Night Trivia

A Jeopardy-style trivia game for a room full of people. The host builds a board of categories and clues, puts the board on a TV or a shared screen, and runs the game from a laptop. Contestants scan a QR code and type their answers on their phones. The host judges each answer: full points, a deduction, nothing, or partial credit for the answer that was nearly right. Double Jeopardy, Final Jeopardy with secret wagers, and team play are all optional.

Boards are saved to your Google account, so you can write one on your laptop tonight and host it from somewhere else next week. You can also draft clues in a spreadsheet and import them, or share a read-only copy of a board with a friend.

Proper Drop

The game here is finding the best pint of Guinness in town. Proper Drop is an installable web app: order a pint, give it a score out of ten, and snap a photo of the pour. If you want to be thorough, break the score down into head, temperature, taste, and texture. Ratings show up on a live feed, and they add up into a bar leaderboard you can filter by country, region, or city.

It was built in a hurry before a pub crawl around St. John's, and it has been used to settle arguments about pints ever since.
